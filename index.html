01 / Profile

I connect civil infrastructure with AI, immersive technology, and geospatial intelligence to improve how we prepare for and respond to disasters.

My work spans rapid post-disaster field reconnaissance, deep-learning-based damage assessment, UAV photogrammetry, geospatial mapping, virtual and augmented reality, and underground infrastructure. I bring together hands-on field engineering and applied research to create systems that are rigorous, useful, and ready for real-world deployment.
